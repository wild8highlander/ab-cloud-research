# Monograph — original v21 edition

The original **v21 edition** is the first public book-length edition of the
AB-Cloud research programme. It is archived, unmodified, under
[`projects/monographs/original-v21/`](https://github.com/wild8highlander/ab-cloud-research/tree/main/projects/monographs/original-v21)
in the repository, in both Russian and English.

## Why an archived edition matters

The archived edition is part of the project's integrity model: later editions
may refine wording, figures and test coverage, but the original text stays
frozen exactly as it was released. Anyone comparing a claim across editions
can trace what changed and what did not.
