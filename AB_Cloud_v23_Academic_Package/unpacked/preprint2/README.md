# `preprint2/` — The Academic Essence Preprint (LaTeX + PDF)
> The 53-page preprint edition of the Academic Essence companion: LaTeX source and compiled PDF, with the addendum integrated.

_Location: [unpacked/](../README.md) › [preprint2/](README.md)_

The **Academic Essence** is the framework's companion account: where the monograph develops the full 38-chapter verification narrative, the Essence compresses the framework into fourteen chapters addressed to the interdisciplinary reader — the physics of the AB-cloud construct, the arithmetic of the ζ dictionary, the tolerance philosophy, and the verification record in overview — with a **bilingual English + Russian annotation**. This folder ships its preprint edition: the LaTeX source (`academic_essence_preprint.tex`, 144 KB) and the compiled PDF (53 pages, September 2026 state, addendum Add.8 included).

## How it relates to the other documents

The Essence shares its quantitative spine with the monograph — same 38 tests, same reference-run numbers, same September 2026 addendum — but re-edited to a shorter, self-contained form suitable for rapid distribution and for readers who meet the work through the preprint servers first. Its compiled length (53 pages) against the monograph's 305 makes it the package's "executive edition": Part I of the monograph maps to Essence chapters 1–5, the verification overview to chapters 6–12, and the addendum to the closing chapters. The v3.2 estimator-audit resolution — the forensic story of the run's one adversarial composite statistic — receives its own chapter here, as does the v1.3 Klein-quartic ensemble pass.

## Compiling

As with the monograph preprint, the source compiles with `tectonic academic_essence_preprint.tex`; the two addendum figures it embeds ship alongside in [`addendum_figures/`](addendum_figures/README.md). The LaTeX is self-contained otherwise, and the bibliography is inline.
