### Research Interests (AB-Cloud programme)

- Hilbert–Pólya hypothesis in computable (numerical) form
- Hofstadter Hamiltonians with topological vortices and Aharonov–Bohm phases
- Random matrix universality (GUE/GOE/Poisson/GSE) and the Montgomery pair correlation
- Topological models of elementary particles (electron/positron as vortices)
- 3D non-Hermitian lattice systems for the Riemann zeros

### Key Contributions

- Construction of the **AB-cloud**: a Hofstadter Hamiltonian whose
  Aharonov–Bohm phases are derived from the non-trivial zeros of ζ(s)
- Verification of GUE-universality of the cloud (⟨r⟩ = 0.5848 ± 0.0260) and of
  statistical indistinguishability from the ζ zeros (Montgomery KS = 0.047)
- Machine-precision topology of the cloud: Byers–Yang defect 3.5·10⁻¹⁵,
  Connes self-duality with 4 zero modes
- Discovery of the unique GUE-consistent spinor structure idx = 38 on the
  Klein quartic (permutation test Z = 14.10)
- Canonical 37-test two-pass Julia verification suite (dependency-free) and an
  independent 10-language verification stack
- 3D lattice laboratory: non-Hermitian Hofstadter Hamiltonian with vortex
  lines ("Universal Lattice Operating System for the Riemann Zeros")
