The ratio (disorder energy q·W) / (kinetic energy t) decides what the vortex cloud does and which spectral statistics come out: • q·W ≳ t (e.g. W=4, q=0.3 → V≈1.2 next to a core): the disorder landscape dominates the vortex motion — vortices pin/steer the eigenstates, states localize on the potential relief, and ⟨r⟩ drifts from GUE (0.5992) toward Poisson (0.3863) by an essentially random amount. That is why these tests measure at W_eff = min(cfg.ab_W, W_max = 1.0): they certify the PHASE physics, not the disorder physics.
