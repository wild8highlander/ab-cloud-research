The critical line in motion: zeros rising, N(T) counting — the E17 flagship as an animation.
N(T) counts the zeros below height T: the copper curve is the Riemann–von Mangoldt main terms, the teal staircase is the actual count. E17 read 649 + 639 zeros blind to T = 1000 — cross-engine disagreement 0.0, bit-exact.
Blind protocol of E17
detectors (EM and Riemann–Siegel) never see the reference → outputs are SHA256-pinned → the validator compares → the verdict quotes the pre-registered thresholds.
What it does not prove
RH above T = 1000; the primality of all zeros; the absence of off-line zeros for γ > 1000. The honest boundary travels with the result.
