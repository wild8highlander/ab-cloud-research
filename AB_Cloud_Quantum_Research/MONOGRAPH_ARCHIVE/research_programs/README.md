# 📜 MONOGRAPH_ARCHIVE — research programs

> **The archival research-program documents: Core, Processor and companions, RU + EN.**

The research-program stream of the archive: `Program_Core_*` and `Program_Processor_*` (with further program documents alongside) in RU and EN, each as PDF + DOCX. These are the *planning* documents of the package — what was intended to be verified and why — and they pair naturally with the campaign register of the main monograph next door: programs state the intentions, the monograph records what actually closed and what stayed open.

As with the whole archive, these documents are frozen; later thinking lives in the current monograph line, not here.
