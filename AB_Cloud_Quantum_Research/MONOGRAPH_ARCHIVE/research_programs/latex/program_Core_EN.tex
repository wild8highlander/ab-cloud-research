\section{Research Program: The Spectral Core}

\noindent\textit{\color{copper}The core program owns the mathematical foundation of the AB architecture: the blind spectrum, null models, the critical line, the trace formula, the obligation machine and branch stitching. Nineteen campaigns and six theorems form a closed loop: construction → certificate → formal statement → independent verification.}

\subsection{1. Program goals}

\begin{itemize}
  \item construct a Hermitian operator H\textsubscript{ξ} whose heat expansion carries the arithmetic of the primes (Λ(n)/√n);
  \item prove the correctness of the construction: Hermiticity (Th1), self-adjointness (Th2), Kato certificates (Th3), the Li formula (Th4), block reduction (Th5), the Guinand–Weil correspondence (Th6);
  \item separate arithmetic specificity from GUE statistics — including honest negative answers on lattices;
  \item fix the RH status with the obligation machine V13 — and keep it blocked while the hypotheses are unproved.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{2. Methodology}

Every campaign starts with a pre-registration protocol: thresholds are fixed before the run. Blind protocols are applied wherever a reference exists (V01: calibration/test with no leak). All derived artifacts (tiles, documents) are built only from committed verdicts. Formal statements are duplicated in Lean 4, Coq, Agda and Isabelle (reductions A1–A6, no sorry/admit).

\subsection{6. Key campaigns in detail}

\subsection{6.1. Six campaigns that defined the core}

\subsubsection{V01 — the blind spectrum}

The campaign is built as a strict no-leak check: the operator is calibrated on 600 ordinates and tested on another 600. The result is dual and both components matter. Positive: the test-set statistics agree with the construction expectations — correlations within control zones. Negative: individual ordinates are not predicted. The practical conclusion: the model works as a consistency counter, not as an oracle; any future claim of "zero prediction" must first refute this verdict.

\subsubsection{V02 — null models and GUE}

The comparison with four null models (M0, N1, N4 and references) over r\_mean, Σ₂, Δ₃ and the KS statistic showed: the AB cloud falls into the GUE class together with the controls (r\_mean 0.6093 versus 0.5837 for random phases, KS 0.0499). Hence the program's methodological rule: GUE agreement is necessary but not sufficient; the arithmetic specificity is sought separately — in the trace, the constants and the behaviour.

\subsubsection{V07 — trace ↔ primes}

The central arithmetic campaign of the core. The heat trace K(x) = Tr e\textasciicircum{}(−xH) expands in a series whose coefficients carry Λ(n)/√n — the content of Th6. The trace-prime correlation r = 0.9996; the lattice control without arithmetic gives 0.76; the gap is the measured specificity. An important side result — the B/C₀ constants erratum: the error was found by an independent derivation and fixed in the same verdict.

\subsubsection{V12 — Kato certificates}

The campaign answers the question "how much can a computed eigenvalue be trusted". Kato theory gives the corridor δ²/gap plus a Gershgorin allowance; numerically the widths are of order 1e−27 at forty significant digits. A rare situation: the error of the estimate is known better than the estimate itself. These certificates later become the basis of the computed status in the obligation machine.

\subsubsection{V13 — the obligation machine}

The executable DAG of the path to RH: five nodes proven, three computed, four assumed (O08, O09, O11, O12); the conclusion is automatically blocked. The verdict note fixes the principle: no numerical check raises the status. The campaign turned the program from a set of results into a system of obligations with checkable statuses.

\subsubsection{V19 — branch stitching}

The final algebraic campaign of the core: the branches of the spectral construction are stitched with a θ-ladder, agreement 0.832 and coherence 0.9995 (z = 43.4); Z₂-monodromy. The certificate is algebraic: going around the branch point twice returns to the original branch. The stitching closes the construction and opens the R2 roadmap: refining r\_align ≤ 0.135 and formalizing the monodromy.
