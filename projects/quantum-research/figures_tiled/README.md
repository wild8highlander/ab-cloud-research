# 🖼️ figures_tiled/ — tiled "4-dimension" figures (600 dpi)

The tiled figures of the AB Cloud Quantum Research package: **each figure is a
2×2 grid of four different measurement dimensions of one theorem** (metric ·
per-instance behaviour · control · threshold). Built from committed campaign
verdicts (`modules/core_spectrum/data/v*_verdict.json`) — no hand-made
numbers. Resolution 600 dpi (6600×4560 px), the *Critical Line & Copper*
style, fully self-contained (no external fonts).

## Files

| File | Theorem | Source campaign |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| `ru/th1_4dim_ru.png`, `en/th1_4dim_en.png` | Th1 hermiticity | V4 |
| `ru/th2_4dim_ru.png`, `en/th2_4dim_en.png` | Th2 self-adjointness | V8 |
| `ru/th3_4dim_ru.png`, `en/th3_4dim_en.png` | Th3 certificates | V12 |
| `ru/th4_4dim_ru.png`, `en/th4_4dim_en.png` | Th4 Li formula | V15 |
| `ru/th5_4dim_ru.png`, `en/th5_4dim_en.png` | Th5 block reduction | V9 |
| `ru/th6_4dim_ru.png`, `en/th6_4dim_en.png` | Th6 trace formula | V7 |

The same figures are duplicated in `theorems_lemmas/T0K_*/figures/` and
embedded into the 12 theorem documents (RU/EN × PDF/DOCX).

## How to regenerate

```bash
python3 scripts/make_tiled_figures.py   # 12 figures × 600 dpi
```

The script reads only the verdicts; when campaigns are updated the figures
are rebuilt from the same data — bit-for-bit for unchanged verdicts
(deterministic seeds).

## Original main figures

All the original figures of the three programs are **preserved unchanged**:
`modules/core_spectrum/figures_hires/` (fig01–fig20 × RU/EN),
`modules/core_spectrum/computations/*/figures/`,
`modules/processor_ab/figures_hires/` (fig01–fig06),
`modules/verifier_multilang/figures_hires/` (fig01–fig14).
The tiled figures are an ADDITION to the originals, not a replacement.

This folder is the **figure shelf** of its module: the publication-grade illustrations (600 dpi where noted) referenced by the module's README, reports and monographs. The figures are regenerated by the module's scripts from the frozen data, and the committed copies serve as the canonical versions that the documents actually embed. Treat them as outputs, not as sources of truth — the truth is the data and the code.

The hub of the tiled "4-dimension" figure family: per theorem, a 2×2 panel showing the metric, the per-gate behaviour, the control and the threshold, at 600 dpi. Language-specific tiles live in `en/` and `ru/`.
