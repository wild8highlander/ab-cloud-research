Hypothesis discipline

The same five rules the program follows — applied to your hypothesis.

  1. Threshold before the run. Fix thresholds in the config; changing them after a run invalidates the verdict.
  2. Blindness. The detector never sees the reference; outputs are SHA256-pinned before validation (CLI).
  3. does_not_prove. Every hypothesis declares what its result does not prove.
  4. Replication. Repeat in a second arithmetic; record the disagreement.
  5. Publish PASS and FAIL alike. The report is an artifact, not a draft.
