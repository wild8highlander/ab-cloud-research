# 🌐 web_lab/ — the AB Cloud Quantum Research portal

`index.html` is the single entry point (RU/EN): cards of the three programs
with links to their web labs, the theorem layer Th1–Th6 (statements,
certificates, reductions) and the summary formal status. Everything runs
locally in the browser, no network.

Following the links:

| Where | What | Tabs |
|------|-----|---------|
| `modules/core_spectrum/web_lab/` | the parent series: Riemann–Siegel Z(t), the gate constructor, trace↔primes, the Li criterion, the ψ-staircase, V16–V19, **Theorems** | 10 |
| `modules/processor_ab/web_lab/` | the processor: ISA, the V20 compiler, zero reading (V23·E11/E17), nonlocality, the roadmap, data, **Theorems** | 8 |

The "Theorems" tab (present in both labs) shows the statements, the
numerical certificates, the paths to the 24 documents and the formal
status with the A1–A6 reduction registry.
