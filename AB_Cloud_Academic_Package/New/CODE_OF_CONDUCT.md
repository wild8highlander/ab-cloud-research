# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as
contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the AB-Cloud
Research Programme and its unified academic package a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards (scientific)

This community has one additional domain-specific standard:

- **Honesty is not optional.** Misrepresenting a numerical result, stripping
  a `does_not_prove` field, or claiming RH where the record says `blocked`
  is a conduct violation of the same severity as harassment: it corrupts the
  shared object we are building — the scientific record.

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism — including of one's theorems
* Showing empathy towards other community members
* Publishing negative results without shame

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome attention
* Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting
* Presenting this programme's results without their honest boundaries

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported by contacting the author through the repository issue tracker. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response
that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
