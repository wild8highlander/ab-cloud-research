# Acknowledgements

The AB-Cloud Research Programme stands on public infrastructure and public
science. The author acknowledges:

- **mpmath** (Fredrik Johansson and contributors) — arbitrary-precision
  ζ-function evaluation, the independent zero oracle in the Hilbert–Pólya
  campaigns (C1, C2, C12).
- **JuliaLang** and its ecosystem (LinearAlgebra, Arblite-adjacent tooling) —
  the language of the blind verification platform (E01–E18) and of the
  38-test heritage run.
- **NumPy / SciPy / matplotlib** — the canonical Python implementation stack
  of the Dirac laboratory and the bridge.
- **Odlyzko's zeros tables** (public data) — the independent zero reference
  used by the spectral core and the animated banner of the source package.
- **Arias de Reyna's** exposition of the Riemann–Siegel formula — the basis of
  the independent second engine (E16).
- The mathematical heritage of **Guinand, Weil, Montgomery, Dyson, Odlyzko,
  Berry, Keating, Connes, Jackiw, Rossi** — the programme's theorems are
  conversations with this literature.
- **Tectonic** (Frank Siegert and contributors) — LaTeX compilation of the
  monographs. **Playfair Display**, **Inter** and **Noto** typefaces —
  the typographic identity of the covers and the web lab.
- The **Zenodo** team — the DOI of record.

No funding was received for this research. No conflict of interest exists.
