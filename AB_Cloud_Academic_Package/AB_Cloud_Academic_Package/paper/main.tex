\begin{abstract}
We report the consolidated state of the AB-Cloud research programme: a
lattice operator carrying $\alpha$ quanta of Aharonov--Bohm flux per
plaquette, engineered into the GUE universality class with an exact layer
(hermiticity, flux quantization, Byers--Yang invariance, chiral pairing)
verified to machine precision. Across 43+ campaigns and 27 theorems with
numerical certificates, the programme establishes the Dirac identity of the
construct's dynamics (continuum limit $R^2 = 0.9997$; Klein tunneling $\times
977$), the presence of the von Mangoldt weight in the heat trace ($r =
0.9996$), and the blind reading of all $\zeta$ zeros to $T = 1000$ by two
independent engines with bit-exact agreement. Spectral identity is not
achieved on any explored configuration; the wall is located in
\emph{locality} itself (subsystem entropies 12--16 nats against Haar-random
160--220). The Riemann hypothesis: the formal obligation machine is
unlocked (v1.2.1) and certifies the path --- verdict \texttt{certified},
zero assumptions between O01 and O12 (the classical Li criterion closes
O12; O08/O09/O11 are certified numerically); RH as a theorem still
requires O11 for all $n$. The programme's
product is the map: any positive construction must be nonlocal or
equivalently entangling.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
If the zeros of the Riemann $\zeta$ function are a spectrum, whose spectrum
is it? The Hilbert--P\'olya programme asks for a Hermitian operator whose
eigenvalues are $1/2 + i\gamma_n$; the Montgomery--Odlyzko statistics
require the GUE class. The AB-Cloud programme builds the candidate on a
finite lattice and measures the gap with pre-registered verdicts.

\section{The construct and what is established}
The AB cloud is an $L\times L$ torus with $\pi\alpha$ flux per plaquette.
Its exact layer is machine-verified; its statistics are GUE-class
($\langle r\rangle = 0.60$ family-wide, $0.608$ for the $\zeta$-decorated
Dirac operator against theory $0.5992$); its dynamics is exactly Dirac.
The trace carries $\Lambda(n)/\sqrt{n}$ with $r = 0.9996$.

\section{The wall}
Spectral identity fails stably: 9--10 orders in configuration metrics,
$\approx 0.5$ order in the trace metric, over 87 gates, 20 blind families
and 121 attractor configurations. The nonlocal isospectral twin achieves
$r_{H7} = 1.000000$, while local lattices suppress entanglement: subsystem
entropies 12--16 nats against Haar 160--220. Locality is the wall.

\section{Honesty}
Nothing here proves the Riemann hypothesis as a theorem: the V13
obligation machine (Lean 4 / Coq) is unlocked and certifies the path
(verdict \texttt{certified} --- zero assumptions on it), while the final
step, O11 for all $n$, remains numerical. Negative results are published as
first-class citizens.
