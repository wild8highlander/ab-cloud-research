This folder is the **figure shelf** of its module: the publication-grade illustrations (600 dpi where noted) referenced by the module's README, reports and monographs. The figures are regenerated by the module's scripts from the frozen data, and the committed copies serve as the canonical versions that the documents actually embed. Treat them as outputs, not as sources of truth — the truth is the data and the code.

The publication figures of the study: heatmaps, ladders and stability panels embedded by the README and the monograph.
