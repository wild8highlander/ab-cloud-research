# 🌀 sections/section6_riemann_zeros — micro-verification

> **One-screen closed-form micro-verification for monograph section 6 (the Riemann-zero layer).**

The section-6 twin of [`../section3_ab_cloud/`](../section3_ab_cloud/): a closed-form, one-screen verification of the Riemann-zero claims made in section 6 of the monograph — Gram-point bookkeeping and the offset identities, checked exactly in [`python/`](python/) against the frozen zero datasets.

As everywhere in `sections/`, the bar is *exactness*: the script asserts its expected output rather than printing numbers for eyeballing, so a silent numerical drift fails loudly instead of passing quietly.
